**Prerequisite:** S01 이미지 마스터 사람 승인 필요. 현재 아직 승인 전이며 이 문서는 실행 대기용.

```text
A handcrafted backlit sand-art animation in warm amber and burnt umber.
The EXACT SAME wall-mounted industrial IP pagephone remains firmly attached
to the wall, with ONE handset resting on its cradle, a rectangular display,
a 3-by-4 keypad, and one rectangular auxiliary box on its right.
The phone hardware, cables, buttons, mounting position and surrounding pipes
remain strictly invariant. Cinematic very slow push-in only.
Subtle individual grains of sand roll and settle around the illuminated edges,
without erasing or morphing the phone. Toward the end, a vague worker shoulder
silhouette enters the left foreground, as if formed by drifting dark sand.
No lip movement, no people speaking, no writing, no captions, no logos.
Generate NO speech, NO music, NO sound. 16:9, exactly six seconds.
```

### Negative / Blockers
`extra handset, changing phone geometry, keypad mutation, device relocation,
realistic camera photograph, live action, subtitles, captions, English voice,
AI-generated dialogue, worker taking handset in S01, excessive dust hiding phone,
industrial alarm`.
